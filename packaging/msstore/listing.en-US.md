## Description (up to 10,000 characters)

Hold Shift while dragging a file and a wheel of formats appears around
your pointer. Drop the file onto a format to save a converted copy next
to the original. Everything runs on your computer, without opening a
separate app or uploading your files to a conversion website.

Mexerica works when dragging files from File Explorer, your desktop, or
file dialogs. There are three wheels:

• Shift: convert. Images (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF, GIF, ICO,
PDF), video (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, animated GIF), audio (MP3, M4A, WAV,
FLAC, OGG, Opus), PDFs to images, text, or Word, Office documents to
PDF, Markdown, and HTML, EPUB e-books, subtitles, and archives (ZIP, 7Z,
RAR, TAR).

• Shift + Alt: tools. Edit images with a live preview, crop to an aspect
ratio, add a background with rounded corners and a shadow, remove
backgrounds, resize images to a maximum file size, read QR codes, trim
video and audio, compress videos to fit within 8, 10, or 25 MB, merge
and split PDFs, and remove metadata (EXIF, GPS, author).

• Shift + Ctrl: actions. Copy file paths, open a terminal in a folder,
copy or move files to another folder, create ZIP archives, and use
Float to keep files in an always-visible bubble for dragging later.

Built into every workflow:
• Existing files are never overwritten: if a name is taken, the new
  file becomes "photo (2).jpg".
• Output is written to a temporary file and renamed only when complete,
  so failed conversions do not leave incomplete output files behind.
• Batch processing: drag multiple files at once.
• Completion notifications with options to show the file in its folder
  or open it.

Mexerica is also available from File Explorer's right-click menu
("Converter com a Mexerica", under "Show more options" on Windows 11),
and through the "mexerica" terminal command.

Office documents are converted using your installed Microsoft Office
applications (Word, Excel, and PowerPoint), preserving their layout.
Without Office, text documents and spreadsheets can be converted to
PDF with their content, without preserving the original layout.

Settings include five color palettes, wheel size, background dimming,
animations, which items appear in each wheel, the output folder, and
whether to start with Windows.

Open source (MIT license): https://github.com/rogersocruz/mexerica

## Product features (up to 20 items, 200 characters each)

1. Hold Shift while dragging a file to open a wheel of formats around your pointer
2. Convert images, video, audio, PDFs, documents, e-books, and subtitles
3. Batch-convert HEIC and AVIF images to JPG or PNG
4. Compress videos to fit within 8, 10, or 25 MB
5. Crop images, add backgrounds with shadows, and remove backgrounds
6. Trim video and audio using a timeline
7. Merge, split, compress, and rotate PDFs
8. Remove metadata (EXIF, GPS, author) from photos
9. Keep your originals intact: existing files are never overwritten
10. Process everything locally: your files stay on your computer
11. Choose from five color palettes and adjust the wheel
12. Open source, with no ads or account required
